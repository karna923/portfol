Mechanical integration

  • Iterated SolidWorks mounts and lightweight string/backpack concepts around a severe mass constraint.
  • Converted uncertain payload capacity into an experimental requirement using incremental bead/wax-chain flight tests.
  • A 3.7 g moth regularly hovered with roughly 250–350 mg lifted and briefly carried 450 mg, supporting a conservative 350 mg design budget.

Signal acquisition

  • Developed the amplifier/filter architecture and a buffered virtual-ground circuit to operate the analog front end from a single supply.
  • Bench-tested gain using a 10 mV input; the 160 Ω configuration produced roughly 4 V output.
  • Used MATLAB for EMG visualization and timing analysis, then integrated the virtual-ground and main amplifier/filter stages. Live-specimen acquisition exposed noise that informed the next electrical iteration.
